## Leander Girrbach
I'm a PhD candidate in the <a href="https://www.eml-unitue.de/">Explainable Machine Learning Group</a> at the <a href="https://www.tum.de/">Technical University of Munich (TUM)</a> and <a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/">Helmholtz Munich</a>. 

Before that, I did my Master's in Computational Linguistics at the <a href="https://uni-tuebingen.de/">University of Tübingen</a> and my Bachelor's in Computational Linguistics at the <a href="https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en">University of Heidelberg</a>.

I'm interested in understanding deep learning models and how their training data shapes their behavior.\
Beyond academia, I'm interested in ancient cultures and languages, especially Chinese and Japanese culture.
